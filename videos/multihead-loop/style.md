# Style guide: Multi-head attention (15 s loop)

**Look: Eight ways of looking.** The same sentence as the attention slide; eight small attention grids, one colour per head, each drawing its own arcs.

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, silent, seamless 15 s loop for a presentation. Follows attention-loop in the series (same palette, same caption list on the left).

**Colours:** one colour per head (`h1`–`h8`); `output` green = the combined output; `line`/`surface` for structure.

**Truth:** the mechanism and numbers (8 heads, dₖ = 64, concat then Wᴼ) are the paper's; heads learning distinct relations is shown in the paper's Figs. 3–5. The specific head patterns here are illustrative, labelled so.
