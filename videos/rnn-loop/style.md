# Style guide: Recurrent neural network (15 s loop)

**Look: Unrolled diagram.** The classic RNN picture: one cell with a loop, unrolled over four steps.

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, silent, for a presentation slide. A seamless 15 s loop: the last frame is the first.

**Colours:** `input` blue = words coming in; `state` amber = the hidden state (memory) carried step to step; `output` green = the predicted next word. `text`/`muted`/`line` for the diagram.

**Motion:** calm, even, mechanical. Signals draw along the arrows; a cell glows when it updates and stays faintly lit while it holds memory. Everything resets together at the end.
