# Style guide: Positional encoding (15 s loop)

**Look: Slots with fingerprints.** One five-word sentence; two words swap places; sine waves under the slots give each position its own pattern.

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, silent, seamless 15 s loop for a presentation. Follows multihead-loop in the series (same palette, same caption list on the left).

**Colours:** `cand` pink = word vectors; `gate` lavender = position signal and its waves; `output` green = attention outputs; `line`/`surface` for structure.

**Truth:** the formula is the paper's (sin/cos at geometric wavelengths, added to the embedding), and self-attention without positions really is order-blind (its outputs only permute). The vectors and the four wave speeds are illustrative, labelled so.
