# Style guide: Attention (15 s loop)

**Look: Who looks at whom.** One sentence; arcs from a word to every other word, thick where it attends.

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, silent, seamless 15 s loop for a presentation. Follows transformer-loop in the series (same palette, same caption list on the left).

**Colours:** `state` amber = queries; `input` blue = keys; `output` green = values and outputs; `gate` lavender = attention weights and arcs.

**Truth:** the mechanism and formula are the paper's (scaled dot-product attention). The vectors and weights are an illustrative example, labelled so.
