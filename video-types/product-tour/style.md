# Style guide: {{title}}

A calm changelog walkthrough. Real footage of the app is the hero: it sits in a large rounded window and the narration points at what changed. Callouts are short (≤ 6 words), appear on their cue beside the footage and leave when the next idea starts.
- `brand` frames the footage and marks app changes. `shipped` green = shipped. `wip` amber = in progress.
- No invented numbers: only ones from the app or the git history.
