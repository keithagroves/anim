# Style guide: {{title}}

**Mood:** curious, clear, calm. A great teacher at a whiteboard, not a sci-fi light show.

**Visual language**
- The hero: (describe the recurring object and what it stands for).
- Colours mean one thing each: `hero` = (…), `accent2` = (…), `good` = (…).
- Text is sparse: one short title per idea and short labels; the narration carries the explanation. No more than ~6 words of free text at once.

**Motion personality**
- Unhurried and precise: `rise-in`, `draw-on`, `pop`, `type-on`. Nothing spins or bounces for decoration.
- Show cause and effect: things grow *from* what acts *to* what it acts on.
- Land beats on narration cues; keep gentle motion between beats; no dead air.

**Don'ts:** no logos or product UIs; no invented numbers.
